Why We Use XIRR

What it is: XIRR (eXtended Internal Rate of Return) is the single annualized rate that makes the present value of every dated cash flow equal to zero. In plain terms, it answers: “what constant yearly return would produce this exact set of payments on these exact dates?”

Why we use it: This strategy’s cash flows don't fall on clean annual boundaries — money goes out on day 1, comes back ~6 months later, and a funded obligation settles ~2 years out. A simple cash-on-cash return (profit ÷ outlay) ignores timing, so it can't fairly compare a 192-day deal to a 365-day deal. XIRR weights every dollar by the exact day it moves. The Y2 obligation is brought to net present value in Year 1 to avoid spikes.

How to read it: An XIRR of 0.0% means the strategy compounds as if it earned that rate per year over its actual holding period — giving full credit for returning money early.
